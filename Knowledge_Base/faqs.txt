FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==========================

Where do I find my loan ID?

On your loan agreement and on every SMS we send you. It looks like KM-L-0123.

I paid but my balance hasn't changed. Why?

Payments take time to reflect: up to 1 business day for mobile money and 2 business days for bank transfers, or up to 5 business days if the reference was missing or wrong. If it has been longer, speak to an agent and have your transaction reference ready.

Can I pay more than my instalment?

Yes. Extra payments reduce your outstanding balance. They do not change your future instalment amount.

How do I get a statement?

Ask a customer service agent. Statements are sent by email or SMS within 2 business days.

How do I change my registered phone number?

Visit any branch with your Omang or passport. For your security, this cannot be done by phone or chat.

Can I get a new loan while I still have one?

You may hold more than one loan if you qualify. You may also apply for a top-up once half of your current loan is repaid.

Do you offer loans to people who are self-employed?

Yes, through the Small Business Loan, if the business has traded for at least 12 months.
