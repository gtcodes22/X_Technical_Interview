ABOUT KOPANO MICROFINANCE
=========================

Kopano Microfinance is a licensed micro lender regulated by the Non-Bank Financial Institutions Regulatory Authority (NBFIRA). We have provided loans to salaried workers, small business owners and families in Botswana since 2014.
