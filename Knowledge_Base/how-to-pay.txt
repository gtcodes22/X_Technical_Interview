HOW TO PAY YOUR LOAN
====================

Always use your loan ID as the payment reference, for example KM-L-0123. You can find it on your loan agreement and on every SMS we send you. Payments without a correct reference take longer to allocate to your loan.

Orange Money
------------

1. Open Orange Money and choose Pay Bill
2. Enter merchant code 5521 (Kopano Microfinance)
3. Enter your loan ID as the reference
4. Enter the amount and confirm with your PIN

MyZaka
------

1. Open MyZaka and choose Pay Merchant
2. Enter merchant number 88031
3. Enter your loan ID as the reference
4. Enter the amount and confirm with your PIN

Smega
-----

1. Open Smega and choose Pay Merchant
2. Enter merchant code 4417
3. Enter your loan ID as the reference
4. Enter the amount and confirm with your PIN

Bank transfer (EFT)

How long payments take to reflect
---------------------------------

Orange Money, MyZaka, Smega: Within 1 business day
Bank transfer: Within 2 business days
Any payment with a missing or incorrect reference: Up to 5 business days

The date we use to decide whether you paid on time is the date the payment was received, not the date it reflected on your loan.

Kopano staff will never ask for your mobile money PIN or banking password.
